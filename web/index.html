Working-Bolt
for the web

Your roster, on the big screen.

Open shifts you can actually take, your month at a glance, who’s on each unit this week, and when your crew is free together, on any laptop in Chrome or Safari. No app or install needed.

Just looking? Walk through every screen with made-up shifts. No sign-in.

Set it up once

Working-Bolt ← drag this button to your bookmarks bar

Chrome

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: ⌘ ⇧ B (Windows: Ctrl ⇧ B).
  2. Drag the green Working-Bolt button onto it.
  3. Sign in to Lightning Bolt as usual, then click the bookmark.

Safari

  1. Show the Favorites bar: ⌘ ⇧ B (or View → Show Favorites Bar).
  2. Drag the green Working-Bolt button onto it.
  3. Sign in to Lightning Bolt as usual, then click the bookmark.

Drag not working? , add any page as a bookmark, then edit it and paste this in as its address (URL). If you click the bookmark on another site, it takes you to Lightning Bolt. Click it again once you’re signed in.

Shift Pool

Every open shift, with the ones that clash with your own days, post-call or time off marked. Shifts offered straight to you stand out.

My Shifts and Today

Two months side by side with your hours, a Today screen of widgets you choose, a calendar file for Outlook or Google, and your own calendars shown next to your shifts.

Who’s On and Crew

The whole week across every unit. Find any colleague in a keystroke, and see the days your crew are all off.

It only reads. Working-Bolt opens on top of the Lightning Bolt page you’re already signed in to and reads your roster with that same session. It never sees your password, and it never accepts, declines or changes anything. To take a shift, it opens Lightning Bolt’s own screen.

Nothing is sent anywhere else. Your choices (theme, crew, widgets, calendars you add) stay in your browser.

Working-Bolt is an independent helper made by the crew. It isn’t made by or affiliated with Lightning Bolt. On your phone, use the Working-Bolt app.
