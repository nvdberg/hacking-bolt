NEW — Busy days (thanks Pieter)
Got STARS, a course or plans on a day you're not rostered? Mark it so you don't pick up a shift by mistake.
• How: in My Shifts, press and hold a day → "Mark busy…" → add a short note (e.g. STARS) → Save. The day shows a grey dashed tag with your note.
• Change it: press and hold the day again → "Edit busy note…" or "Clear busy".
• In the Pool: a shift on a busy day says "You're busy · STARS" instead of "Available" and drops out of "For me" (still listed under "All"). Tap it and the app asks "Take it anyway?" before going on to Lightning Bolt.
• Your Lightning Bolt time-off requests (pending or approved) count as busy too.
• Stored on your phone only — never sent to Lightning Bolt, the server or anyone else. It comes back with an iPhone backup restore.

NEW — Is the Pool up to date? (thanks Brian)
• "Updated 11:23" next to the Pool tabs shows when the list was last checked. Pull down to refresh.
• "Recently taken" at the bottom of the Pool lists shifts picked up in the last 2 days (unit, day, how long ago — no names). If a shift disappears from the Pool, you'll see it was taken, not lost. Tap the line to open or close it.
• Don't want it? Tap the eye to hide it. Bring it back with "Show recently taken" at the bottom of the Pool, or More → Advanced.
